Section 01 · Motivation

From wearable signal
to line-safety response

Accelerometers and gyroscopes in a waist-worn phone record how a worker moves. Every 2.56 seconds the signal is cut into a window, summarised as 561 numbers, and labelled as one of six activities. A robot cell can then slow down for walking workers, and a supervisor is alerted within seconds when someone appears to be lying on the floor.
